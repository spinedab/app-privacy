DannCarros is a marketplace for buying and selling vehicles directly between private individuals and sales advisors. We encourage you to read this policy before using the app.

2. What data we collect and why

Using DannCarros as a buyer does not require creating an account: you can browse the inventory and contact the owner via WhatsApp without registering or providing us with any personal data. The collection of personal data occurs primarily in the seller/advisor flow (registration and publishing of vehicle listings).

We collect the following data, all for the purpose of making the app work:

About location: the location permission is optional and is only used, if you expressly grant it, to add an area reference (coordinates or area name) to a listing you are creating. The app does not track your location in the background nor share it with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not process payments within the app: there is no payment gateway or in-app purchases. Publishing plans are handled outside the app, so we do not collect card data or financial information.
  • We do not perform tracking across other apps or third-party websites. The app includes no advertising SDKs or advertising identifiers and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.
  • We do not collect health data, contacts, calendar, browsing history, audio, or diagnostic data for third parties.

3. Legal basis and purpose of processing

We process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of the service: account data (name, email, session token) and listing data (photos, contact phone number, description) are necessary for you to register, publish your vehicle, and connect with buyers.
  • Consent: access to the camera, the photo library, and location only occurs after your explicit authorization on the device. You may revoke these permissions at any time from your operating system settings.
  • Legitimate interest: maintaining the security of the service and preventing abuse or fraudulent use of the marketplace.

4. Third parties and subprocessors

DannCarros minimizes its reliance on third parties. The services involved in the processing of data are:

  • Own backend (autos.574.lat): a PHP/MySQL server operated by DannCarros and hosted on hosting infrastructure (cPanel). It stores seller/advisor accounts and listings. It is responsible for storing your data. More information: https://autos.574.lat.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms, Inc.): contact between buyer and owner takes place through WhatsApp. When you tap the contact button, the app opens WhatsApp with the owner's number; from that point on the conversation is governed by the WhatsApp/Meta policies, which are outside of DannCarros. Privacy policy: https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy.
  • Apple App Store: distribution of the app and, where applicable, downloads, take place through Apple. Privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for commercial or advertising purposes.

5. Data retention

We retain your data for as long as your seller/advisor account remains active and for as long as your listings are available on the marketplace. When you request the deletion of your account or of a listing, we delete the associated data from our active systems within a reasonable period, unless we are required to retain certain information for legal obligations or to resolve disputes. Data from buyers who only browse and make contact via WhatsApp is not stored on our backend.
