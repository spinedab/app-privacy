2. What data we collect and why

The app collects only the data needed to operate the home-delivery service. Each type of data, its source, and its purpose are detailed below:

2.1. Driver data (app user)

DataSourcePurpose
Name Registered by the store administrator Identify the driver to the dispatch center and to the customer during delivery.
Phone number Registered by the administrator; used by the driver to sign in Authentication (sign-in with phone number + PIN).
Vehicle type and internal identifier (driverId) Registered by the administrator / stored on the device Operations and order assignment.
Precise GPS location (latitude, longitude, heading, speed, accuracy, and time) Device sensor, only when the driver turns on "online" mode Coordinate deliveries and show delivery progress to the dispatch center and the customer.
About your location. Your precise GPS location is shared only while you turn on the "online" switch on the app's main screen. You can go offline at any time using that same switch, and the app will stop sharing your position. Location use is limited to delivery operations: it is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not shared with third parties for tracking across apps or companies. Your location is linked to your identity (name and driver identifier) for operational purposes only.

2.2. Customer data (third parties) shown for each order

To complete deliveries, the app displays and lets the driver update information about Papelería Bolivariana's customers. This data is entered by the store (administrator), not by the driver; the app only shows it to the assigned driver and lets them update the order status.

  • Customer name
  • Customer phone number (to call or message on WhatsApp with one tap)
  • Delivery address and neighborhood (to open the route in Google Maps)
  • Order details: products, total to collect, payment method, and tracking code

The sole purpose of this data is to enable delivery of the order. The driver must keep it confidential and use it only to complete the delivery.

2.3. What we do NOT collect

  • We do not use advertising or ad networks.
  • We do not use analytics tools or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect advertising identifiers (IDFA) or perform cross-app tracking.
  • We do not sell personal data to third parties.

3. Legal basis and purpose of processing

We process the data described above for the following purposes and on the following legal bases:

  • Performance of the employment or service relationship with the driver: authentication, order assignment, and delivery coordination.
  • Legitimate interest and operation of the home-delivery service: sharing location during the workday (only in "online" mode) to coordinate and show progress to the customer.
  • Fulfillment of the service to the end customer: processing the order data (customer name, phone number, and address) needed to deliver the requested purchase.

Processing is limited to what is strictly necessary for the app's functionality ("App Functionality").
