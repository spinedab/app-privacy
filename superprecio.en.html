2. What data we collect and why

You can use price search and comparison without creating an account. We only collect identifiable personal data if you choose to sign up for the "SuperPrecio Club." The table below details what data is processed and for what purpose:

Data When it is collected Purpose Linked to your identity?
Email address Only if you create an account / sign in Create and manage your Club account; service communications Yes
Name Only at sign-up Identify you within your account Yes
User identifier (account ID) Only if you create an account Associate your favorites and preferences with your account Yes
Approximate location Only if you grant the location permission Infer your country and show local prices and currency No
Search terms Each time you run a search Query stores and providers and return offers to you No

Club account (optional)

Signing up is free and entirely optional. If you create an account, we collect your email address and your name, and our backend assigns you a user identifier. This data is linked to your identity and is used to give you access to favorites, alerts, and Club benefits. You can use all search and comparison features without signing up.

Approximate location (only to infer your country)

With your permission, the app requests your approximate location (coarse, not fine precision) for the sole purpose of inferring your country and showing you local prices and currency (Colombia, Panama, Dominican Republic, Mexico, Peru, Ecuador). Location is processed on your device and stored locally; it is not sent to our servers linked to your identity and is not used for advertising or any kind of tracking. If you deny the permission, the app still works: you can choose your country manually, and Colombia is used by default.

Searches

The terms you search for are sent to our backend to query stores and providers and return offers sorted by price. These queries do not include your user token, meaning they are not linked to your identity. In addition, your search history is stored locally on your device so you can review it.

On-device AI assistant (optional)

The app includes an optional artificial-intelligence assistant that runs directly on your device ("Edge AI"). If you choose to enable it, you download an AI model once (data, not executable code). When you use this assistant, your searches are processed locally and are not sent to the cloud for that feature. It is entirely optional, and the app works perfectly without it.

Pharmacy and medication price comparison

SuperPrecio compares prices of pharmacy products and medications as part of its catalog. We work exclusively with pricing and availability information: the app does not provide medical advice, does not recommend dosages, and does not sell medications directly. We do not ask for or collect health data, medical records, or prescriptions. Searches for pharmacy products are treated just like any other price-comparison search described above.

What we do NOT do: we do not process payments or card data within the app; we do not use advertising, attribution, or analytics SDKs; we do not track you across third-party apps or websites (App Tracking Transparency: no); and we do not sell your personal data.

3. Legal basis and purpose of processing

  • Performance of the service / consent: we process your email, name, and user ID to create and manage the Club account that you voluntarily request.
  • Consent: we process your approximate location only if you grant the system permission; you can revoke it at any time from your device settings.
  • App functionality / legitimate interest: we send your search terms to the backend so we can provide the price comparison, which is the app's core purpose.
